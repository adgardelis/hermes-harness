# Provenance and naming

HERMES HARNESS is an independent Hermes-native project.

Its design research studied DeepSeek Harness (DSH), released under the MIT License, and Hermes Agent, also released under the MIT License. HERMES HARNESS selectively reimplements and hardens ideas within Hermes Agent's native architecture; it does not bundle or run the DeepSeek Harness runtime.

- DeepSeek Harness: research provenance and comparative design source.
- Hermes Agent: target runtime and upstream contribution destination.
- HERMES HARNESS: the canonical name of this Hermes-native context and tool-result economy system.
